The Science of Spatial Capture

What Are Photogrammetry Services?

Photogrammetry is the science of obtaining measurements and spatial information from photographs. Images captured using drones, aerial cameras, or other imaging equipment are processed using specialized software to identify dimensions, surface features, elevations, and spatial relationships.

This process helps convert raw imagery into useful digital assets, including 2D maps, 3D models, orthophotos, digital elevation models, and point clouds. These outputs allow professionals to visualize locations, examine terrain, assess existing conditions, and support project planning with detailed visual information.

At TRISET Solutions, our approach focuses on making complex spatial data easier to understand and more useful across different industries.

Comprehensive Capabilities

Our Photogrammetry Solutions

Structured spatial outputs engineered for engineering, planning, measurement, and visualization:

Capability 01

1. 2D Mapping and Cartography

Accurate maps are essential for land development, infrastructure planning, and geographic analysis. Our 2D mapping services transform aerial and other suitable imagery into detailed map outputs that represent roads, buildings, boundaries, and other visible features. These maps help organizations understand site conditions and support location-based planning.

Capability 02

2. 3D Modeling and Reconstruction

Our 3D photogrammetry services convert overlapping photographs into digital representations of real-world objects, structures, and landscapes. These models help users visualize physical environments from different perspectives, understand spatial relationships, and communicate project details more effectively. They can support engineering, construction, infrastructure documentation, and visualization workflows.

Capability 03

3. Orthophoto and Image Mosaicing

An orthophoto is an aerial image corrected for geometric distortions so that it can be used for mapping and spatial analysis. Through orthophoto generation and image mosaicing, multiple images can be combined into a consistent, detailed visual representation of an area. These outputs are useful for land-use assessment, site monitoring, urban development, and project documentation.

Capability 04

4. Digital Elevation and Terrain Models

Understanding the shape and elevation of land is important for construction, drainage planning, environmental studies, and infrastructure development. We support the creation of digital elevation models (DEM) and related terrain products from suitable spatial datasets. These models help professionals examine elevation differences, terrain characteristics, and surface conditions for further analysis.

Capability 05

5. Point Cloud Generation and Processing

Point clouds consist of collections of three-dimensional data points that represent the surfaces and shapes of physical environments. Photogrammetry can generate point clouds from overlapping images, providing detailed spatial information for visualization, measurement, and 3D reconstruction. Depending on the project, these datasets can support engineering, mapping, and digital modeling applications.

Capability 06

6. Drone Image Processing and GIS Integration

Drone imagery provides a flexible way to collect visual information across large areas and difficult-to-access locations. Our drone image processing capabilities help convert captured imagery into structured spatial outputs. When integrated with Geographic Information Systems (GIS), this information can support geographic visualization, spatial analysis, land assessment, and infrastructure planning.
